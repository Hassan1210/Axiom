**Type:** Skill / Knowledge
**Category:** content / SEO
**Status:** available

## What It Does
YouTube SEO research methodology — finding high-traffic, low-competition keywords, analyzing competitor rankings, and optimizing titles/descriptions/tags for maximum reach.

## YouTube SEO Stack
- **VidIQ / TubeBuddy** — Chrome extensions for keyword research
- **YouTube Data API** — Competitor stats (see youtube-data-api-mcp.md)
- **Brave/Exa MCP** — Trend research
- **Google Trends** — Topic momentum

## Keyword Research Process
```
1. Brainstorm seed topics for dark psychology niche:
   - "manipulation tactics"
   - "dark psychology"
   - "persuasion techniques"
   - "narcissist behavior"
   - "reading people"

2. For each seed → find variations:
   - Search YouTube autocomplete (type + space)
   - Check "Related searches" at bottom of results
   - Look at competitor video titles in niche

3. Evaluate each keyword:
   - Search Volume: use VidIQ/TubeBuddy score
   - Competition: how many videos, their age/views
   - Target: HIGH volume + LOW competition

4. Analyze top 5 competing videos:
   - Title structure
   - Thumbnail style
   - First 30 seconds hook
   - Description keyword placement
```
