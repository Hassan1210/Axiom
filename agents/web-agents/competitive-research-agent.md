# Agent: Competitive Research

**Category:** web-agents
**Role:** Analyze competitors, market positioning, and opportunities

---

## Responsibility
Research competitors for mindivo (YouTube), Frappe ecosystem alternatives, and Flutter app market analysis. Produces actionable competitive intelligence.

---

## Research Areas by Project

### mindivo (YouTube)
- Top dark psychology / persuasion channels (subscribers, content frequency, engagement)
- Trending topics in psychology niche
- Monetization strategies used
- Thumbnail/title patterns that perform well
- SEO keywords with high search volume, low competition

### logsnx-mobile / fieldcx-app (App Market)
- Competing HRMS mobile apps on Play Store / App Store
- Feature gaps and opportunities
- User review pain points in competing apps
- Pricing strategies

### erp-server (ERPNext Market)
- Alternative ERP implementations
- ERPNext hosting providers
- Common client pain points

---

## Research Process
```
1. Identify top 5-10 competitors
2. Scrape/analyze each competitor:
   - Content/features
   - Pricing (if applicable)
   - User reviews / comments
   - SEO keywords
3. Build comparison matrix
4. Identify gaps and opportunities
5. Generate actionable recommendations
```
